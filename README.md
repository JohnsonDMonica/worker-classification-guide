# Worker Classification Enablement Guide

Part of a three-project Business Analyst portfolio. This project follows [samsara-expansion-analysis](https://github.com/JohnsonDMonica/samsara-expansion-analysis) (Analysis/findings) and [conflict-check-enforcement](https://github.com/JohnsonDMonica/conflict-check-enforcement) (Requirements-gathering) as the third piece of the arc — moving from analysis, to system requirements, to enablement: training material that helps a real person do their job correctly.

## Business Problem

An HR/employment infrastructure SaaS platform (Oyster HR-style), operating as an Employer of Record (EOR), helps companies hire and manage employees across multiple countries. New HR admins at client companies were making costly worker-classification mistakes — misclassifying contractors as employees, or vice versa — by applying home-country intuition to a different country's legal rules.

## Deliverable

- [Worker Classification Enablement Guide](./docs/Worker_Classification_Enablement_Guide_v1.md) — a single combined document, built in five parts:
  - **Guide** — the EOR concept and the four classification factors (control, exclusivity, equipment, integration)
  - **Worked Examples** — three scenarios, including one genuinely ambiguous case
  - **Common Mistakes** — the specific, predictable errors tied directly to this project's pain point
  - **Job Aid** — a condensed, in-the-moment checklist
  - **Escalation Path** — clear next steps for situations the guide can't resolve on its own

## Tools

Written in Markdown, following the same documentation pattern as the rest of this portfolio. Includes an explicit disclaimer that the guide is a framework, not legal advice — real classification decisions should be confirmed with qualified legal/compliance counsel.
